AnglerBook

A fly-fishing logbook for iOS · GLYSK OÜ

AnglerBook is a private logbook for fly fishers. It remembers the things a good session is made of — where you were, the conditions, what rose, what you tied on, and what you landed — so your record builds itself into something worth looking back on.

What it remembers

Every outing captures the session (date, water, GPS, weather and conditions), the catches (species, size, the fly that worked, a photo), and the gear you fished — rod, reel, line, leader and tippet — drawn from a structured gear catalog so logging stays quick and consistent.

Your fly box & gear

Keep your patterns and equipment organised, see what actually produces over a season, and turn a run of sessions into insight rather than a pile of scattered notes.

AnglerBook Pro

AnglerBook Pro unlocks the full picture — the complete record and the deeper features, for anglers who want everything in one place. Choose a one-time 3-day pass (€1.99) or 7-day pass (€4.99), or an auto-renewing Monthly (€7.99) or Annual (€29.99) plan. Billing is handled by Apple through your App Store account.

Private by design

Your log stays on your device and, if you choose, in your own iCloud account. No content servers, no third-party advertising or analytics trackers. See the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.

Platforms

AnglerBook is built for iPhone on iOS 26, with Live Activity and Dynamic Island support. An Android version is planned.
